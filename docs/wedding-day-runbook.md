# Wedding-Day Runbook

## Before guests arrive

1. Connect the slideshow laptop to power.
2. Connect the projector or television.
3. Disable laptop sleep and automatic updates.
4. Connect to venue Wi-Fi.
5. Confirm the mobile hotspot backup.
6. Open `/slideshow` and enter browser full-screen mode.
7. Scan the printed QR code with an iPhone and an Android phone.
8. Upload a test photo batch.
9. Confirm the batch appears in the gallery.
10. Confirm the photos enter the slideshow queue.
11. Download one original from the open-photo view.
12. Remove the test photos through the authenticated emergency admin page.
13. Upload several suitable starter photos so the screen is not empty.

## During the wedding

- Keep the slideshow tab open.
- Keep the laptop connected to power.
- Do not keep the AWS console on the public display.
- Use the emergency admin page only when a photo must be removed.
- If venue Wi-Fi fails, switch the laptop and moderator phone to the hotspot.
- Do not change infrastructure during the event unless the system is unusable.

## At the end

- Confirm thank-you mode is active or open `/slideshow?thankyou=1`.
- Keep the slideshow cycling while guests leave.
- Verify the total photo count.

## Within 2–3 days

- Download all original photos.
- Create two local backups.
- Disable public upload endpoints.
- Disable public gallery access.
- Rotate or remove organizer credentials.
- Review AWS spending.
- Keep the private photo archive until the family confirms receipt.
